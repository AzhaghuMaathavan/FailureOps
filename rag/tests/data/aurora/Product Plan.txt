Document: Aurora Expense Tracker - Product Plan & Roadmap
Organization: Aurora Technologies Inc.
Confidentiality: PRIVATE

1. Strategic Objective & Target Audience:
The goal of Aurora Expense Tracker is to deliver automated receipt parsing, bank synchronization, and real-time expense approvals for mid-market finance teams. Target activation rate is 60% of trial signups.

2. Release Milestones & Delivery Schedule:
- Alpha Architecture Complete: March 15, 2026
- Beta Launch: Originally scheduled for May 1, 2026. However, due to critical CI/CD pipeline instabilities and engineering fatigue, the beta launch has been postponed by 4 weeks to June 1, 2026.
- General Availability: Q3 2026

3. Security & Compliance Constraints:
Mandatory SOC2 and banking partner compliance requires all users to complete a 7-step identity verification and bank credential connection before accessing the dashboard.
